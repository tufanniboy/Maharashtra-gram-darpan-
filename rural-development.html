Rural Development Initiatives

Empowering villages through sustainable infrastructure, healthcare, sanitation, and community-led growth.

Transforming Rural India through "Jan Bhagidari"

Established in 1998 under the vision of Shri. Ravindra Himmattrao Deshmukh, Maharashtra Gram Darpan focuses on the socio-economic upliftment of the underprivileged. Our philosophy is rooted in "Jan Bhagidari" (People's Participation), believing that true development happens only when the community takes ownership.

We operate extensively across Amravati, Akola, Gondia, and Hingoli districts in Maharashtra, with expanding initiatives in Madhya Pradesh and Arunachal Pradesh.

Recognition: Our cooperative institute 'Yuva' received the First Award from the Government of Maharashtra for excellence in village sanitation and public awareness campaigns.

Key Projects & Interventions

Maha Arogya Shibir

Healthcare

Organized mega health camps in 17 villages conducting 48 shibirs. We provide free surgeries for brain, heart, and eyes, along with free medicines and glasses distribution.

Silt-Free Dam & Fertile Shivar

Agriculture & Environment

Implemented in Majri Mhasla village (Nandgaon Khandeshwar). Removing fertile silt from dams increases water capacity while providing nutrient-rich soil to farmers, boosting crop yields significantly.

Shankar Pat

Culture & Community

A grand cultural event organized since 2023-24 to foster community bonding and relieve agrarian stress. It serves as a platform for exchanging agricultural knowledge, with participation from multiple districts.

Plastic-Free Village & Waste

Sanitation (SBM)

Under Wastewater and Solid Waste Management, we are transforming villages into "Plastic-free zones" and implementing "Waste to Fertilizer" initiatives to reuse polluted water.

Sanitation Infrastructure

Health & Hygiene

Established production centers for economic latrines at Amravati and Tiwsa. We actively construct school latrines and dedicated facilities for women to ensure hygiene and dignity.

Green Highway Initiative

Environment

Successfully implemented a massive tree plantation drive covering 40km of highway. This project contributes to environmental conservation, soil stability, and green cover enhancement.

Partner with Us for Rural Growth

We collaborate with government bodies, CSR initiatives, and individuals to drive impactful change in rural India. Join our mission today.

ग्रामीण भारत का परिवर्तन

1998 से, महाराष्ट्र ग्राम दर्पण ग्रामीण परिवर्तन में सबसे आगे रहा है। संस्थापक श्री रवींद्र हिम्मतराव देशमुख की दृष्टि से हम "जन भागीदारी" में विश्वास करते हैं। हम अमरावती, अकोला, गोंदिया और हिंगोली जिलों में सक्रिय रूप से काम करते हैं।

प्रमुख उपलब्धि: हमारे सहकारी संस्थान 'युवा' को ग्राम स्वच्छता और जन जागरूकता अभियानों में उत्कृष्टता के लिए महाराष्ट्र सरकार से प्रथम पुरस्कार मिला।
